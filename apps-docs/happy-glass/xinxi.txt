应用名称:
 Happy Glass

一句话描述：
Happy Glass: The glass is sad because it's empty! Draw lines freely to guide water, fill it up and make it smile again. Be creative, think out of the box, and strive for 3 stars in increasingly challenging levels.

产品详情：
Happy Glass is a popular casual physics puzzle game loved by players worldwide. The glass is lonely and sad without water. Use your finger to draw lines freely on the screen to build reasonable paths, cleverly avoid all kinds of barriers, and let the water pour into the glass smoothly. Plenty of well-designed levels with gradual difficulty bring endless challenges. It is easy to play, full of fun, perfect to relax your mind and exercise your logical thinking anytime and anywhere.
